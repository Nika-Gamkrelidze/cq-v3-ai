SUPPORT POLICIES (SAMPLE)

SUPPORT HOURS
Support is available 24/7 by phone and chat. Email tickets are answered within 4 business hours. Outside business hours, critical issues are handled by the on-call team.

REFUND POLICY
Refunds are available within 14 days of purchase. The customer contacts support with the order number; processing takes 3–5 business days. Partial refunds are possible for unused subscription months.

SERVICE PLANS
Basic includes up to 10 users. Business includes up to 50 users and priority support. Enterprise includes unlimited users, a dedicated manager and a service-level agreement. Yearly billing saves 20%.

ESCALATING AN URGENT ISSUE
A customer can say "urgent escalation" to any agent, or mark a ticket Critical. A supervisor replies within 30 minutes. Escalations are never closed without customer confirmation.

DATA STORAGE
All customer data is stored in EU data centres and never leaves the region.

COMPLAINTS
A complaint can be submitted by phone, chat or email. It is registered within one business day and answered in writing within 10 business days. The customer is told the reference number at once.

CHANGING CONTACT DETAILS
Contact details can be changed in the customer portal, or by asking an agent after identity verification. The change takes effect immediately and a confirmation is sent to the old and new address.

PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
On public holidays support works from 10:00 to 18:00. Critical issues are still handled around the clock by the on-call team. Email tickets received on a holiday are answered on the next business day.
